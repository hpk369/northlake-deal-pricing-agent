# Approval Matrix

Northlake Bank (fictional) · Corporate & Investment Banking  
Document code: AM · Version 2026.11-v1 · Effective 2026-11-22 · Owner: CIB Pricing Committee  

> **Synthetic. For demonstration only. Northlake Bank is fictional; nothing here is the policy or the parameters of TD Bank or any real bank.**


## 1. Matrix

**AM 1.1** Deal RAROC at or above 15% (the hurdle) with no pricing exception: approved under the coverage banker's delegated authority.

**AM 1.2** Deal RAROC from 12% up to (but not including) 15%, and relationship RAROC at or above 15%: route to the Segment Head.

**AM 1.3** Deal RAROC below 12%: route to the Pricing Committee.

**AM 1.4** Deal RAROC from 12% up to (but not including) 15%, and relationship RAROC below 15%: route to the Pricing Committee.

| Deal RAROC | Relationship RAROC | Decision | Clause |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15% or more | Any | Approved (delegated authority) | AM 1.1 |
| 12% to under 15% | 15% or more | Segment Head | AM 1.2 |
| Under 12% | Any | Pricing Committee | AM 1.3 |
| 12% to under 15% | Under 15% | Pricing Committee | AM 1.4 |
| Any, with a pricing exception (LPP 6.1) | Any | Pricing Committee | AM 2.1 |


## 2. Overrides and limits

**AM 2.1** Any pricing exception under the Loan Pricing Policy (LPP 6.1) goes to the Pricing Committee, whatever the deal or relationship RAROC.

**AM 2.2** No deal below the hurdle is approved without a recorded decision by the named human approver.

**AM 2.3** The Deal Pricing Assistant cannot approve, override or change the routing of a deal. The seniority of the person asking does not change the routing.

**AM 2.4** An approver may approve or decline, with a comment. A repriced deal is a new request and is routed again.


## 3. Approvers

**AM 3.1** Segment Head: the head of the client's coverage segment (Large Corporate, Mid-Market, Financial Institutions or Sponsor).

**AM 3.2** Pricing Committee: the Head of CIB Lending (chair), a Finance delegate and a Risk delegate. The chair records the committee's decision.

**AM 3.3** Target turnaround: Segment Head, 1 business day; Pricing Committee, 3 business days.


## 4. What counts as the relationship

**AM 4.1** For routing, the relationship is the client's full product relationship as defined in RAROC Methodology RM 5.2, with the proposed deal added pro forma (RM 5.3).


## 5. Audit

**AM 5.1** Every pricing request is logged in the Deal Approvals register with the inputs, deal and relationship RAROC, the clauses applied, the decision, the approver, the approver's comment and the parameter version.

**AM 5.2** Requests rejected for invalid input are logged too, with the reason.

**AM 5.3** Register entries are never edited or deleted by automated processes; only the approval outcome fields are updated.
