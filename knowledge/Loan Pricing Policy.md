# Loan Pricing Policy

Northlake Bank (fictional) · Corporate & Investment Banking  
Document code: LPP · Version 2026.11-v1 · Effective 2026-11-22 · Owner: CIB Pricing Committee  

> **Synthetic. For demonstration only. Northlake Bank is fictional; nothing here is the policy or the parameters of TD Bank or any real bank.**


## 1. Purpose and scope

**LPP 1.1** This policy sets the minimum pricing terms for committed lending facilities (revolving credit facilities and term loans) offered by Northlake Bank's Corporate & Investment Banking coverage teams.

**LPP 1.2** It applies to new facilities and to amendments that change the spread, fees or tenor. It does not apply to bonds, derivatives or advisory mandates.

**LPP 1.3** Meeting this policy is necessary but not sufficient: the deal must also clear the 15% RAROC hurdle (RAROC Methodology RM 6.1) or be approved by the approver named in the Approval Matrix.


## 2. Definitions

**LPP 2.1** Spread: the margin over Term SOFR, in basis points (bp) per year, charged on drawn amounts.

**LPP 2.2** Upfront fee: a one-time fee paid at closing, in bp of the total commitment.

**LPP 2.3** Commitment fee: an annual fee, in bp, charged on the undrawn part of a revolving facility.

**LPP 2.4** Internal rating: Northlake's 1 to 10 obligor rating scale, where 1 is the strongest credit and 10 the weakest.

**LPP 2.5** Tenor: years from closing to final maturity, including any extension options at the client's discretion.


## 3. Minimum spreads

**LPP 3.1** Minimum spreads (floors) apply equally to revolvers and term loans and depend on the client's internal rating band and the facility tenor.

**LPP 3.2** Minimum spread over Term SOFR for facilities with a tenor of up to 3 years, by internal rating band. For example, the minimum spread for a rating 5 or rating 6 client on a 3-year revolver is SOFR + 110 bp.

| Internal rating band | Minimum spread, tenor up to 3 years |
|---|---|
| Rating 1 to 2 | SOFR + 60 bp |
| Rating 3 to 4 | SOFR + 85 bp |
| Rating 5 to 6 | SOFR + 110 bp |
| Rating 7 to 8 | SOFR + 160 bp |
| Rating 9 to 10 | SOFR + 250 bp |

**LPP 3.3** Tenor add-ons: for tenors over 3 years and up to 5 years, add 15 bp to the LPP 3.2 floor; for tenors over 5 years and up to 7 years, add 30 bp. For example, a rating 5 or 6 client on a 5-year term loan has a minimum spread of SOFR + 125 bp.

| Internal rating band | Up to 3 years | Over 3 to 5 years | Over 5 to 7 years |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rating 1 to 2 | 60 bp | 75 bp | 90 bp |
| Rating 3 to 4 | 85 bp | 100 bp | 115 bp |
| Rating 5 to 6 | 110 bp | 125 bp | 140 bp |
| Rating 7 to 8 | 160 bp | 175 bp | 190 bp |
| Rating 9 to 10 | 250 bp | 265 bp | 280 bp |

**LPP 3.4** The floor applies to the spread at closing and to every level of any margin grid; a rating-linked step-down must not take the spread below the floor for the client's rating at that time.


## 4. Fees

**LPP 4.1** New facilities must carry an upfront fee of at least 15 bp of the commitment.

**LPP 4.2** Revolving facilities carry a commitment fee on the undrawn amount. The standard commitment fee is 25 bp per year, and the pricing tool uses it unless the banker gives another figure.

**LPP 4.3** For RAROC purposes the upfront fee is spread evenly over the tenor (RAROC Methodology RM 3.2).


## 5. Tenor limits

**LPP 5.1** Tenor should match the client's financing need and the useful life of what is being financed.

**LPP 5.2** Extension options at the client's discretion count toward tenor (LPP 2.5).

**LPP 5.3** The maximum tenor for a revolving credit facility is 5 years.

**LPP 5.4** The maximum tenor for a term loan is 7 years.


## 6. Exceptions

**LPP 6.1** Pricing below a floor in section 3, a fee below the minimum in section 4, or a tenor above a maximum in section 5 is a pricing exception.

**LPP 6.2** Every pricing exception requires Pricing Committee approval, whatever the deal's RAROC (Approval Matrix AM 2.1). A deal with an exception is never approved under delegated authority.

**LPP 6.3** An exception request must state the business rationale (relationship, competitive or strategic) and the cross-sell expected from the client.


## 7. Governance

**LPP 7.1** The Pricing Committee reviews this policy at least once a year.

**LPP 7.2** All numbers in this policy come from one parameter set (version 2026.11-v1). The pricing tool, the RAROC Methodology and this policy must use the same version.
