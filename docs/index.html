How it works

  1. The agent extracts the terms and asks for anything missing in one question.
  2. A Power Automate flow does all the maths. Deal and relationship RAROC, pricing floors and the approval matrix are expressions in the flow, never the language model.
  3. Below the 15% hurdle, a human decides. The deal goes to a Segment Head or the Pricing Committee through Power Automate Approvals. The agent can't approve or override.
  4. Everything is logged to a Dataverse audit table: inputs, both RAROC figures, policy clause, decision, approver and comment.
  5. Policy answers cite the clause from three synthetic policy documents.
